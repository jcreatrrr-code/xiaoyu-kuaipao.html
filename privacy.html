Xiaoyu Kuaipao (小鱼快跑) · Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Xiaoyu Kuaipao does not collect, upload or share any personal information.

  • No sign-up or login, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress is stored only on your own device and is removed when you delete the app.
  • The app works fully offline.
  • The app asks for access to your photo library only when you take a photo in the fishing mode and choose “Save Image”. We never see that photo.
